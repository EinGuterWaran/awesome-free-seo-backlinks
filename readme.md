## Why Free Backlinks Still Work in 2026

Backlinks remain one of the strongest ranking signals in Google's algorithm. Submitting your website, SaaS product, AI tool, or startup to **high-authority directories** builds foundational link equity, diversifies your backlink profile, and gets your brand crawled by AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity) that increasingly source recommendations from authoritative directories. The key is **quality over quantity**: a handful of listings on DR 70+ sites with complete profiles beats hundreds of thin submissions to spammy link farms.

## How to Use This List

1. **Start at the top.** Work through the DR 90+ and DR 80–89 sections first. These pass the most link equity.
2. **Prioritize dofollow.** Entries marked ✅ dofollow pass PageRank directly; ➖ nofollow links still add brand visibility, referral traffic, and profile diversity.
3. **Match your niche.** Use the Category column to find AI tool directories, SaaS review sites, B2B platforms, or general web directories that fit your product.
4. **Submit gradually.** Spread submissions over several weeks with unique descriptions per listing to keep your link velocity natural.
5. **Track everything.** Log submission date, status, and live URL in a spreadsheet so you can verify indexing later.

## FAQ

**Do directory submission sites still help SEO in 2026?**
Yes, when done selectively. High-DR, niche-relevant directories provide foundational backlinks, referral traffic, and citations that search engines and AI assistants use to establish trust. Avoid bulk submissions to low-quality directories.

**What is the difference between dofollow and nofollow backlinks?**
Dofollow links pass link equity ("link juice") and directly influence rankings. Nofollow links do not pass equity but still drive referral traffic, build brand awareness, and make your backlink profile look natural.

**What is a good Domain Rating (DR) for backlink sources?**
Anything above DR 50 is solid; DR 70+ is excellent. This list includes 18 sources at DR 90+, the same tier as Medium, G2, and SourceForge.

**How many directories should I submit to?**
Start with 10–20 high-quality, relevant directories and expand gradually. Complete, unique listings on fewer sites outperform mass submissions.

**Are these backlink sites really free?**
Yes, every source on this list has a free submission option. Some offer optional paid or featured tiers.
